## Prompt A

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished, realistic professional UI design presentation for NEON PLAYER Android landscape video playback. This is a NEW design concept, not a code screenshot. Wide landscape image, about 3:2 canvas. Graphite matte background. Upper 65%: one complete landscape phone screen, straight-on, very thin unobtrusive bezel, showing a beautiful realistic cinematic mountain lake and cabin video on the LEFT 76% and a solid near-black playback control panel on the RIGHT 24%, clearly separated, no controls on the actual picture. Same panel geometry for comparisons: inner left part of panel is a refined vertical rotary camera-focus-style scroll wheel with curved fading tick marks and a fixed central small time capsule "24:18"; total "1:42:06" understated. Outer right part contains a LARGE circular play/pause control EXACTLY at 50% of the phone screen height, 10-second rewind immediately above and 10-second forward immediately below. Other actions arranged as separate small blocks ABOVE and BELOW this central transport: top gear/settings and brightness-volume sliders, bottom speed "1×", queue/playlist, image-fit corners. Tiny NEON PLAYER branding at top; absolutely do not put playback near the upper third. Secondary icons are translucent soft grey at approximately 50-60% opacity, primary play remains clear and legible with restrained turquoise accent. All symbols geometrically clean, consistent visual weight, immediately recognizable, large touch areas. No harsh glowing outlines, no cyan everywhere, no weird hand-drawn wobbly glyphs. Lower 30%: neatly aligned enlarged icon specimen sheet in two rows, with small readable Russian function captions, showing this SAME icon style for: play, pause, rewind 10, forward 10, volume, brightness, settings, queue, image fit, screen rotation, lock, unlock, audio/subtitle tracks, speed. Tracks appear in specimen sheet only, not as an extra playback panel button. Small title at upper left. No unrelated screens, no full home menu, no desktop clutter, no implementation labels, no watermarks. Preserve large breathing room and beautiful typographic hierarchy. TITLE text exactly "A · LINE". STYLE A: premium round-stroke minimalist icons, consistent approximately 1.7px strokes at 24px, smoothly rounded ends, balanced open negative space, elegant purposeful outlines. Large primary pause symbol inside a thin turquoise circular outline with near-black center; rewind arcs perfectly round and symmetrical. Secondary actions have no enclosing button borders except a subtle touch surface if useful. Clean restrained Deck aesthetics, vector-sharp, no material shine. Specimen icons larger and brighter than the in-player secondary icons for inspection.
